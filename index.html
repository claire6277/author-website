Behind the Story

Meet CJ

CJ Maylock is an Ohio author and educator who writes science fiction, cosmic horror, comedy, and sapphic stories about ordinary people confronting impossible circumstances.

Her work is shaped by classrooms, friendship, identity, absurd humor, and people trying to keep going when the universe refuses to behave.

When she is not writing, CJ teaches students with autism, creates YouTube videos, and laughs at videos of basset hounds.
