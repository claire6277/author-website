THE FEATURED NOVEL

The English Department
vs. Chaos and Nonsense

Sage Lyken is a high school English teacher who thought the worst part of her day was grading essays that were clearly written by AI. That changes when interdimensional rifts begin opening around the school.

With the help of her two coworkers, Sage must close the rifts, protect her students, uncover what is happening, and somehow still have her lesson plans submitted by Monday morning.

ABOUT CJ

The ordinary collides with the impossible.

CJ Maylock is an Ohio author and educator who writes stories where the ordinary collides with the impossible.

Inspired by classrooms, cosmic horror, science fiction, and absurd humor, her work explores friendship, identity, and what it means to keep going when the universe itself seems determined to fall apart.

When she is not writing, CJ teaches students with autism, creates YouTube videos, and laughs at videos of basset hounds.

Answers about the book, its genres, publication status, and future updates.

The novel follows high school English teacher Sage Lyken as interdimensional rifts begin opening around her school. Sage and her two coworkers must close the rifts, protect their students, and uncover the mystery behind them while surviving the ordinary demands of teaching.

It combines science fiction, fantasy, cosmic horror, teacher and workplace comedy, mystery, character-driven drama, and sapphic romance.

The horror is cosmic, eerie, and strange rather than focused on gore or jump scares. The book balances unsettling ideas with absurd humor and emotional relationships.
