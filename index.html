Three English teachers. Interdimensional rifts. Lesson plans still due Monday.

Sage Lyken is a high school English teacher at Northdown High School. Alongside fellow teachers Courtney Johnson and Nathan Brooks, she is pulled into the orbit of the Federal Investigation of Interdimensional Anomalies after a dangerous encounter at school.

The novel blends workplace comedy, cosmic horror, friendship, mystery, and the exhausting reality of protecting students while reality itself refuses to cooperate.

Behind the story

Meet CJ Maylock

CJ Maylock is an Ohio author and educator who writes science fiction, cosmic horror, comedy, and sapphic stories shaped by classrooms, friendship, identity, absurd humor, and people trying to keep going when the universe refuses to behave.

When she is not writing, CJ teaches students with autism, creates YouTube videos, and laughs at videos of basset hounds.

“When nonsense is the only thing that makes sense.”
